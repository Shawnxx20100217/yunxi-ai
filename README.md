## 主要功能 · Features

- **AI 悬浮窗 / Floating AI workspace**：切换 ChatGPT、Gemini、Grok、DeepSeek。<br>Switch between ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok and DeepSeek.
- **截图上传 / Screenshot attachments**：框选或一键全屏截图，直接添加到网页聊天框。<br>Capture a region or a full screen and attach it directly to the web chat.
- **文字识别 / Text recognition**：截图识字、获取选中文字，直接填入网页。<br>Extract text from screenshots or selected text and insert it into the web chat.
- **灵活显示 / Flexible window controls**：调节透明度、置顶，在全屏应用上方显示，支持右上角唤出。<br>Adjust opacity, keep the window on top, display it over full-screen apps and reveal it from the top-right corner.
- **截图归档 / Screenshot archive**：按日期和时间命名，自动保存到桌面「云隙 AI 截图」文件夹。<br>Automatically save screenshots with date-and-time filenames to the “云隙 AI 截图” folder on your Desktop.

## 安装使用 · Getting started

1. 下载并解压安装包，打开「安装或更新云隙 AI」，点击「安装并打开」。<br>Download and extract the installer, open “安装或更新云隙 AI” (Install or Update Yunxi AI), then click “安装并打开” (Install and Open).
2. 首次打开时复制设备识别码，联系管理员获取激活码。<br>On first launch, copy your device ID and contact the administrator for an activation code.
3. 完成激活，登录需要使用的 AI 平台即可使用。<br>Activate the app, then sign in to the AI services you want to use.

截图需要「屏幕录制」权限；读取选中文字可能需要「辅助功能」权限。请把权限授予主程序「云隙 AI」。各 AI 平台的账号和订阅需自行准备。

Screenshots require Screen Recording permission; reading selected text may require Accessibility permission. Grant these permissions to the main “云隙 AI” app. Accounts and subscriptions for each AI service are separate.
